The problem

When a table closes mid-service, a booking must not be silently moved — the guest’s time and accepted terms are the promise.

What the service guarantees

  • Closure replans are previewed by a manager, never applied silently.
  • Applying a plan is atomic: closure recorded and bookings moved together.
  • Moved bookings keep their time, owner, party size, and accepted terms.

Why it is hard

  • IANA timezones and DST make “the same clock time” non-trivial.
  • Retries must be idempotent — a double submit must not double-book.
  • Policies, revisions, and recurring series all interact with every change.

The product

Search → book → replan → promise kept

Search & book
Availability grid, single or combined tables, timezone-correct slots.
Closure preview
Manager previews a deterministic repair plan for a closed table interval.
Atomic apply
Closure + moves commit together; affected bookings gain a reassigned entry.
Guest view holds
Availability and lookup reflect the new table — same time, same terms.

Booking surface

  • Auth, search, create, amend, move, cancel, lookup
  • Combinable table pairs for larger parties
  • Idempotency-Key safe retries

Management surface

  • Effective-dated policies and revisions
  • Recurring series with owner-only time amendments
  • Reservation history and terms, end to end

Not claimed
